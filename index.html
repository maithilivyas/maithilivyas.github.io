Projects

01. Ancient Temple Cinematic

Unreal Engine 5 | Level Design | Cinematic

A cinematic sequence featuring a Nataraja statue inside an ancient cave. I designed the environment layout, adjusted lighting and fog for a mystical ambiance, and utilized post-process volumes for visual tone.

02. Japan Station

Unreal Engine 5.3 | Lumen | Real-Time Rendering

A real-time cinematic inspired by Japanese nightlife. I focused on mood and atmosphere using directional lighting and reflection captures to simulate wet asphalt. The scene features Niagara rain particles and thunder effects.

03. VR Museum Experience

Meta Quest | Spatial Storytelling | Interaction

A fully immersive VR museum. I architected the structure using modular assets and programmed complex interactions, allowing users to physically grab and inspect artifacts using hand-tracking physics.

05. Third Person Shooter

Combat Mechanics | UI/UX | AI Agents

A complete shooter framework focusing on AI combat. Enemies utilize pathfinding to engage the player. I also designed the UI/HUD for health systems and the "Game Over" logic flow.
